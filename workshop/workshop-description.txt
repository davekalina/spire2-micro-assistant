Micro Assistant by realtruegravy

This mod exists to try and improve your micro by highlighting useful information during combat.

What does the mod do exactly?  Basically it's stuff like this:

- Card Trigger Indicators.  If a card in your hand will increment a counter or trigger an effect, the mod will remind you directly above the card.  For example: a skill that will increment or trigger Tuning Fork, or a card with Exhaust that will trigger Feel No Pain, etc.
- Proc and Break Warnings.  When you go to PLAY a card that will either proc an effect (e.g. Kunai, Ornamental Fan, Pen Nib) or break an effect (e.g. Pocketwatch, Orichalcum) the mod will let you know with a handy popup!
- Passive Block Previews.  The current total block from your Orichalcum(s), Cloak Clasp, Ripple Basin is all previewed at once!  Sort of similar to that other useful mod (hat tip to Avisolei)... but this one works for anything that passively blocks.
- Relic Reminders (for counting relics, setup relics, first-play relics, etc).  Don't forget to setup your Happy Flower(s)!  Nib your Pen!  Joss your Paper!  Tune your Fork!  An indicator at the top of the screen will always remind you of the specific relics that need this kind of careful attention.  You know, the fun stuff.
- End of Turn Reminders.  Right on top of the End Turn button, any active effects that trigger on the turn ending (like Pael's Eye or Ripple Basin or Art of War) will remind you of their existence (and let you know if you've forgotten about them and that their effects have been cancelled).
